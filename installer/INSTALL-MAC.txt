Mosaic - macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)

1. Close your DAW. Copy the plugins into your plug-in folders (Finder: Go > Go to Folder...):
      "Mosaic.vst3"       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
      "Mosaic.component"  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (Logic, GarageBand)
   "Mosaic.app" is the standalone version: drag it into Applications.
2. This early build is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it until you allow it once.
   Open Terminal and paste:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Mosaic.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Mosaic.component /Applications/Mosaic.app
   (For the app alone you can instead open it, then System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.)
3. Rescan plugins in your DAW. Ableton Live: Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan. Logic checks Audio Units on its next start.
4. Put Mosaic on an instrument track and play. Mosaic-Manual.pdf is the instruction manual.
